## Direction contract

THESIS: A costing tool for a pastry maker that refuses the spreadsheet — no cell grid, no row-and-column data table, no tiny tappable cells — because the user pinned exactly that: *"no tiene que parecer excel… mucho menos en el teléfono."* The category default is a pastel dashboard of rounded white cards with a cupcake mark; its predictable opposite is a dark "pro kitchen ops" console with neon figures. Both are refused. Instead the surface is built from the kitchen's own surfaces: enamel fields with hard black rims, set into cement grout. Each cost section is a field you can see the edges of, not a region of a sheet.

OWN-WORLD: Argentine *baldosa calcárea* and *enlozado*. Flat saturated glaze fields, each bounded by a hard 2px near-black rim exactly as an enamel bowl's lip is: teal `#0F5C5B` owns ingredientes, ochre `#C2721A` owns mano de obra, oxblood `#8C2B21` owns packaging y extras, graphite `#17181A` owns the total. Ground is cement `#EDE6D8`, rules are grout `#C9BFAC`. Three values only per field: glaze, rim, and the cream the type sits in. No gradient, no shadow, no glass, no border-radius beyond the 2px a real rim has. Type is Archivo and Archivo Expanded — a grotesque drawn in Buenos Aires, with true tabular figures — set large, because the numbers are the content. A quarter-tile cement motif appears only in the page's quiet corners and in empty states, never behind data.

STORY: She arrives with nothing — no ingredients, no recipes — so the first thing the surface does is invite her to put something in, not display a dashboard of zeros. She adds an ingredient in a few taps, builds a recipe line by line watching the total move, sets a margin she understands because the app tells her in words what a 100% markup actually earns her, and leaves with a price she can paste into WhatsApp. Later, when butter goes up, she changes one price and every saved recipe re-prices itself.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On the phone, a cement ground with the recipe's name and `rinde` as a single enamel header strip, then one full-width teal `INGREDIENTES` panel whose lines are stacked cards — ingredient name large, `cantidad` and its unit on the second line, the line's own cost right-aligned hard against the rim — with a single wide `+ Agregar ingrediente` detent at the panel's foot. A fixed graphite rail sits on the bottom edge carrying the running `COSTO TOTAL` in Archivo Expanded at display size and the primary action; it never scrolls away. On desktop (1280–1920) the panels stack in a left column at a held measure while the graphite total box pins to the right as a standing tile, so the number is always in view at every width. No horizontal scrolling at any size, ever.

FORM: Baldosa y Enlozado — candidate 6 of my seven grounded directions, which is the roll's assignment; seed key `3e5ef73e`, scope direction, mode operate. Raised by four named donations before presentation. From the datamatics challenger, **tabular numeric discipline**: real tabular figures and every money column aligned on the decimal so nothing dances while she types. From the vertical-feed challenger, **thumb-rail ergonomics**: a persistent bottom rail on phones carrying the running total and the primary action, reachable without moving the hand. From the cassette j-card challenger, **declared capacity made visible**: every line carries its own cost tight against the column edge, and `rinde` acts as a capacity the per-portion figure is measured against continuously rather than only at the foot. From the Factory catalog challenger, **catalog numbering as the navigation spine**: every saved recipe carries its N° and date, and the history is a sequential index that doubles as the app's map. Brief-pinned constraints that beat the roll and bind every later edit: it must not read as a spreadsheet, it must be comfortable to look at, and the phone is the hard case, not the afterthought.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
